This policy explains what information [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], doing business as One Air Maverick ("One Air Maverick," "we," "us"), collects when you use this website or request a flight, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to requests you send us through it.

We collect only what we need to plan and operate your flight and to reply to you. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the flight request form, or contact us by email or phone, you may share:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Trip details: departure and destination, dates, preferred times, one-way or round trip, and whether your dates are flexible.
  • Who is traveling: the number of adults and children, and any pets.
  • Anything you add in the notes, such as luggage, accessibility needs or a meeting you need to make. Please share only what you are comfortable with.

If you book a flight, we will also ask for the information that aviation, security and border authorities require for each passenger, such as full legal name, date of birth, gender, and passport or other travel document details for international flights. We collect that separately, when it is needed, and not through this website's request form.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Cloudflare, Inc.], records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Settings stored on your device. The site saves your light or dark display choice, and your analytics choice if we ask for one, in your browser's local storage. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Analytics, only with your consent. If we turn on website analytics, we will ask first, and nothing is loaded unless you accept. Analytics would measure things like which pages are visited, and we do not send your name, email, phone or trip details to analytics. [If analytics stay off, delete this bullet. The site's map and airport search run entirely in your browser from files on our own server. What you type into them is not sent anywhere unless you submit a flight request.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your request, plan your flight and recommend the right aircraft.
  • To operate your flight safely and lawfully, including preparing passenger manifests and meeting FAA, TSA, U.S. Customs and Border Protection and foreign government requirements.
  • To communicate with you about your trip, including changes, weather and schedule updates.
  • To keep business records that the law requires of an air carrier, and to handle billing, disputes and legal claims.
  • To protect the website, our customers and our operations from fraud and misuse.
  • To send occasional updates about our service, only if you ask for them. You can stop them at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep flight requests that do not become bookings for up to [24 months], then delete them. For flights we operate, we keep passenger and trip records for as long as aviation, tax and other laws require, which can be several years. You can ask us to delete a request sooner.

How we protect it

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We limit access to your information to the people who need it to plan and operate your flight. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you where the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it by law, for example records of a flight we operated.
  • Stop sending you updates.

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Texas, Virginia and other states with privacy laws, you may have additional rights, such as the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither) and the right to appeal a decision we make about your request. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it, and you may use an authorized agent.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track you across other websites. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing of your information.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. Children may travel with us, and a parent or guardian provides their details when booking.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in a more direct way, such as by email.
