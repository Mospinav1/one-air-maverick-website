          Frequently Asked Questions

          I have never flown private. What should I expect?
          A calm, simple day. You arrive a short time before departure, meet your crew and board. There is no security line or gate. We walk you through every step before you decide, and nothing is confirmed until you say so.
          I already fly private. What is different here?
          The same crew every time, three aircraft suited to different trips, and a flight planned around your schedule rather than a fleet's.
          Where do you fly?
          From and to anywhere in the United States and the Caribbean, including smaller airports close to your destination.
          Which aircraft do you fly?
          The Dassault Falcon 200, a 9-seat midsize jet for longer and faster trips; the Beechcraft 1900D, a 19-seat twin turboprop with a stand-up cabin for larger groups and shorter runways; and the Turbo Commander, a 6-seat twin turboprop for small parties and regional trips. We recommend the right one for your trip.
          How is pricing determined?
          By route, aircraft and trip details. You see what is included before you decide.
          How do you approach safety and maintenance?
          Each aircraft follows a documented maintenance program and inspection schedule, and every flight follows checklists and go and no-go rules. You are welcome to see the records.
          Can I meet the pilot and see the aircraft first?
          Yes. Before your first flight you can meet your pilot, walk around the aircraft and ask anything.
          Can I bring family or colleagues or my dog?
          Yes. Tell us who is coming when you plan the trip.
          What if my plans change?
          Let us know as early as you can and we will adjust the timing or airports.
          What happens if the weather is bad?
          Safety decides. If conditions are not right, we do not fly, and we help you find the best alternative.
          Is my information private?
          We use what you share only to plan and operate your flight and to reply to you. We do not sell it. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
